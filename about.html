Who We Are

Built for businesses that want technology to simply work

Vistaar Infotech Pvt. Ltd. is an emerging sales and services company based in Mumbai, built for businesses that want technology to simply work. We handle IT infrastructure from end to end — selecting the right products, installing them properly, and keeping them running with dependable after-sales support.

The name Vistaar comes from the Hindi word विस्तार, meaning expansion. It reflects both what we do for our clients — helping their technology infrastructure grow with their business — and the ambition that drives our own company forward.

We are a single-window partner. Whether you need a firewall configured, a server room built, cloud infrastructure set up, or a fleet of laptops deployed, you call one team. No juggling multiple vendors. No finger-pointing when something goes wrong. One point of accountability, always.

Vision, mission & values

Vision

To be the most trusted single-window partner for technology sales and services, known for honest advice and dependable after-sales care.

Mission

To help businesses expand without technology holding them back — by supplying genuine products, installing them professionally, and supporting them with fast, fairly priced service.

Our core values

01
Genuine products only

We never supply counterfeit, grey-market, or end-of-life products. Every item we sell comes through authorised channels with proper documentation and warranty.

02
Transparent pricing

You receive a written quotation before any work begins. No hidden charges, no surprise invoices. If scope changes, we tell you before we proceed — not after.

03
Commitments in writing

Response times, project timelines, and delivery dates are put in writing. If we commit to something, we mean it — and if something changes, we communicate early.

04
Long-term relationships

We build businesses, not transactions. Our goal is to be the team you call first when technology decisions arise — year after year, not just when there's a problem.
