Privacy Policy

How Vistaar Infotech collects, uses, and protects your information.

⚠ Legal Review Required: This is a draft privacy policy. It must be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before the website goes live. Ensure it reflects actual data processing practices, applicable Indian law (IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023), and any third-party services (analytics, form handlers) in use.

Last updated: October 2026  |  Effective: Upon website launch  |  Company: Vistaar Infotech Pvt. Ltd., Mumbai, Maharashtra, India

1. Introduction

Vistaar Infotech Pvt. Ltd. ("Vistaar Infotech", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website at vistaarinfotech.com (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit the Website or use our contact forms.

By accessing the Website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

When you submit the enquiry or quote request form, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Company name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service interest (selected from a dropdown)
  • Message text
  • Any optional file attachment you choose to upload

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider may automatically log:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL
  • Date and time of access

Analytics: The Website may use Google Analytics to understand visitor behaviour. This service uses cookies and collects anonymised usage data. Analytics is not active until a Measurement ID is configured and user consent obtained. [Update this paragraph once analytics is live.]

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Provide the services you have requested
  • Send information relevant to your enquiry
  • Improve our Website and services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

This Website may use cookies — small text files stored on your device. Cookies may be used for:

  • Essential website functionality
  • Analytics and performance measurement (if configured)
  • Remembering your consent preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality. A cookie consent banner will be displayed when analytics is active.

5. Data Storage and Security

Enquiry data submitted through the contact form is transmitted to our designated email address. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information, including using HTTPS for data transmission. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

Form integration: The contact form backend is not yet configured. Until a form submission service is connected, enquiry data remains on the client device only and is not transmitted to our servers. This will be updated before launch. [Remove this notice once the form backend is live.]

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as necessary to respond to and fulfil your request, and for a reasonable period thereafter for business and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

8. Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.
