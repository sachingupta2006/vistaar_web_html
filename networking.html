Networking & Firewall Services

Networking and firewall services for reliable, secure offices

From LAN design and structured cabling through to firewall hardening and VPN — we plan, install, and test every layer of your network.

What We Deliver

Complete networking, from the cable to the cloud edge

We design your network correctly from day one — preventing the expensive rework that comes from shortcuts.

LAN, WAN & Wi-Fi Design

Full network design covering wired LAN, internet WAN links, and enterprise Wi-Fi. We plan coverage, capacity, and segmentation before a single cable is pulled.

Firewall Configuration & Hardening

Palo Alto, Fortinet, Cisco, and Juniper firewall setup and hardening. Rule reviews, policy clean-up, and threat prevention enabled properly.

VPN Configuration

Site-to-site and remote-access VPN setup for secure connectivity between offices and for remote workers. IPSec, SSL-VPN, and SD-WAN options.

Switching & Routing

Managed switch configuration, VLAN segmentation, QoS, and routing protocols. Clean, documented configurations that are easy to support long term.

Network Audits & Troubleshooting

Systematic assessment of existing networks to find security gaps, performance issues, and configuration errors. Full audit report with prioritised remediation steps.

Structured Cabling

Cat6/Cat6A cabling installation, patch panels, and cable management — tested, labelled, and documented to professional standards. Suitable for new offices and upgrades.
