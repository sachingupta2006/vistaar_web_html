For example, if we simplify the individual service pages or remove the FAQ sections, I can bring the cost down. But I'd rather keep the full scope because I believe it will genuinely serve your business better."

### "Another developer quoted us less."

> "That's possible — and I'd encourage you to compare carefully. The key questions to ask any developer are: Will they build it from scratch for your business, or use a template? How many pages are included? Is mobile design included? What happens after the website is delivered? Is post-launch support included? Once you compare apples to apples, the difference in value usually becomes clear."

> "Also — you've seen the prototype. Judge by what's already built, not just by price."

### "We need some time to decide."

> "Absolutely — I respect that. Can I ask what specific concerns or questions you need to resolve before making a decision? [Listen.] I want to make sure you have all the information you need. If it would help, I can send you a written summary of today's meeting, including the scope and pricing, so you have something to refer to."

### "Can you do it for half the price?"

> "At half the price, I'd have to significantly reduce the scope. I could deliver a basic 4–5 page website without the individual service pages, brand detail, or contact form functionality. That would cost around ₹14,000–₹16,000. But looking at what you've seen today, I genuinely don't think a basic site serves your business well. A half-built website still needs your time and energy. Would you like to explore which specific parts we prioritise?"

### "Can you include hosting for free?"

> "Hosting is a third-party cost — it's like asking a builder to include the land for free. I can help you set up the hosting account, recommend the right plan, and configure everything — that support is included. But the actual hosting fee goes directly to the hosting company. It's not something I can absorb."

### "Can we pay after the entire project is completed?"

> "I understand the concern — you want to see the finished product before paying. But I'm sure you'll agree that's not a practical business arrangement. I need to invest significant time and resources upfront. The milestone payment structure I've proposed is fair: 40% to start, 30% after your review and approval, and the final 30% at launch. This means you only pay the last instalment when you're satisfied the website is ready to go live."

### "We are not sure whether we need a website."

> "Let me answer that with a question. If a potential client is referred to you today and the first thing they do is Google 'Vistaar Infotech' — what do they find? If the answer is nothing, or something underwhelming, you've lost credibility before the first conversation even happens. The question isn't really whether you need a website. The question is whether you want to make a strong first impression or not."

### "We want more features within the same budget."

> "I'd love to include everything. But scope and budget are always connected. Let me write down the additional features you'd like and I'll give you a clear view of what each one costs to add. That way you can decide which are priorities. I won't agree to take on more work for the same money — that leads to rushed work and neither of us ends up happy."
